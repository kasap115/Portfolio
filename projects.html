02

S / N Computing

Personal project · In development

A PC-building planner for the Kosovo market. You choose the parts, the site checks that they fit together, then finds the cheapest shop for each one.

  • Guides the user through three steps: plan, build and buy.
  • Offers ready-made builds by budget and use (Full HD, 1440p, 4K and quiet systems) that load straight into the builder.
  • Compares every part's price across four Kosovo shops (Gjirafa50, Startech24, Buton Electronics and Point Computers KS) and shows the cheapest basket, even when it is split between shops.
